# Privacy Policy

**Effective date**: June 26, 2026

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the Vault app ("we", "our", or "the App") handles information. We minimize data collection and protect user privacy. This policy applies when you download, install, or use the App.

## Information we collect

- No collection in production: The App does not collect personal data in the production release. There are no advertisements, trackers, or analytics collecting personal information in production.
- Beta testing only: During beta testing we requested an email address to collect feedback and provide support. That beta-only feedback feature will be removed before the App's first production release.
- Temporary Firebase usage: For beta builds we used Firebase for crash reporting and limited diagnostics to improve quality. Firebase usage is limited to beta/testing builds and will be removed prior to production deployment.
- Data on device: All user data created or stored by the App remains on the user's device. If you enable iCloud backup on your device, your App data may be included in iCloud backups — that is controlled by your device settings and Apple, not by the App.

## How we use information

- Beta feedback: Any email addresses or feedback collected during beta testing were used only to communicate with testers and to improve the App.
- Crash reports (beta): Crash and diagnostic reports used during beta were used to fix issues and improve stability. These are limited to the beta/testing context and will not be part of the production App.

## Third-party services

During beta testing we used Google Firebase for crash reporting and limited diagnostics to assist with debugging. Firebase is a third-party service with its own privacy practices. No third-party advertising or tracking SDKs are present in the production App.

## Data storage and retention

- Local storage: User data is stored only on the user's device unless the user chooses to back up their device to iCloud.
- Retention: Beta feedback and any associated contact information were retained only as long as necessary to address feedback and fix issues. If you were a beta tester and wish to request deletion of your beta data, contact us using the developer contact information in the App Store listing.

## Advertising and tracking

The App does not contain advertising. We do not profile users for advertising purposes and do not engage in cross-app tracking.

## Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information to us, contact the developer contact in the App Store listing so we can remove that information.

## Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the data stored on the device. Because no system is completely secure, we cannot guarantee absolute security of local data.

## Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the Effective date at the top of this document. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

## Contact

If you have questions, requests for data deletion, or other concerns about privacy, please use the developer contact information provided on the App Store listing for this App.

## Acknowledgement

By using the App, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.
